# 📊 ASEAN DSE Analytics: Dashboard Statistics Guide

Welcome to the ASEAN Data Science Explorers Master Analytics Dashboard. This guide explains every statistic, chart, and metric displayed in the app and provides strategic context on how to use them to craft a winning storyboard.

The dashboard is built around a comparative philosophy: **Overall Regional Trends** vs. **The Winners' Playbook**. By analyzing the "deltas" (differences) between average participants and podium finishers, you can reverse-engineer a winning strategy.

## 🎛️ The Multi-Dimensional Filters (Sidebar)

The sidebar allows you to slice the dataset to find highly specific niches.

- **Year:** Track how competition metas change over time (e.g., what won in 2017 vs. 2024).
    
- **Country:** Find out what topics dominate specific national finals.
    
- **Target Beneficiary:** Isolate a specific group (e.g., "Farmers") to see exactly what technologies and solutions are being built for them across the region.
    

_(Note: The dashboard features an automated data-cleaning engine that fixes AI categorization errors in the background, ensuring your filtered charts are always perfectly accurate.)_

## 1. Top-Level KPIs (The Bird's-Eye View)

These metrics sit at the top of the dashboard and provide a snapshot of the current dataset based on your active filters.

- **Total Storyboards Analyzed:** The total number of valid PDF submissions successfully processed.
    
- **Unique Countries Represented:** The footprint of the competition across the 10 ASEAN member states.
    
- **Most Targeted SDG & Most Popular Solution:** The most common goals and formats. _Strategy tip: If you are building the "Most Popular" solution, you must work twice as hard to stand out._
    

## 2. 🏛️ Historical Trends

These two charts provide macroscopic context on the competition's history.

- **🏆 The Hall of Fame:** A bar chart displaying the historical performance of different ASEAN countries, strictly filtering for teams that placed 1st, 2nd, or 3rd at the Regional Finals.
    
- **📈 The Tech Evolution:** A line chart showing the momentum of different technology stacks over the years. Look for the lines curving sharply upward—these represent emerging technologies that judges are currently excited about.
    

## 3. The 7 Core Metrics (Symmetrical Tab Analysis)

The heart of the dashboard lives in the tabs. We display the exact same charts for the _Overall_ pool and the _Winners_ pool. Here is how to interpret each one:

### 🎯 Most Targeted SDGs (Horizontal Bar Chart)

- **What it is:** A ranking of the specific UN SDGs the teams are trying to solve.
    
- **The Delta:** If average teams heavily target SDG 4 (Education) but winners heavily target SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption), you know that environmental sustainability pitches are currently resonating better with the judging rubric.
    

### 👥 Primary Beneficiaries (Pie Chart)

- **What it is:** Who the solution is designed to help (e.g., Urban Populations, Rural Communities, MSMEs).
    
- **The Delta:** Finding a "Blue Ocean." If 60% of teams are building solutions for Urban Populations, that space is crowded. Building a solution tailored for Healthcare Workers instantly makes your pitch unique.
    

### 🛠️ Preferred Tech Stacks (Vertical Bar Chart)

- **What it is:** The digital tools teams integrate into their solutions (e.g., Data Analytics, AI, IoT, Blockchain).
    
- **The Delta:** Data Analytics is a baseline requirement. However, if the Winners tab shows a massive spike in "AI / Machine Learning" compared to the Overall tab, it proves that the judges award top points to teams pushing the technological envelope.
    

### 📱 Types of Solutions (Horizontal Bar Chart)

- **What it is:** The tangible output of the pitch (e.g., App, Hardware, Policy/Framework).
    
- **The Delta:** Almost everyone pitches a Mobile App. But if you see "Policy/Framework" making up a significant chunk of the Winners tab, it means judges appreciate scalable, systemic regulatory solutions, not just smartphone interfaces.
    

### 🧩 SDG Complexity Index (Vertical Bar Chart)

- **What it is:** A count of _how many_ different SDGs a single team attempts to tackle simultaneously.
    
- **The Delta:** Do average teams focus deeply on 1 SDG, while winners interlink 3 or 4 SDGs? If winners have a higher complexity index, it shows that judges favor holistic solutions that understand how socio-economic problems are connected.
    

### 🤝 Team Formation (Pie Chart)

- **What it is:** Indicates whether the two team members come from the _Same University_ or _Different Universities_ (Cross-Institution).
    
- **The Delta:** If cross-institution teams make up only 10% of the overall pool but account for 40% of the winners, it strongly suggests that combining different academic perspectives creates a much stronger, more well-rounded pitch.
    

### 🗣️ Top Buzzwords (Horizontal Bar Chart)

- **What it is:** A Natural Language Processing (NLP) analysis that strips out common words and counts the most frequent, meaningful vocabulary used in the teams' executive summaries.
    
- **The Delta:** This teaches you how to speak the judges' language. If winning teams constantly use words like "sustainable," "circular," "ecosystem," and "platform," you should organically weave those concepts into your own storyboard and script.
